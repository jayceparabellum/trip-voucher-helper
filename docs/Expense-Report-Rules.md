# Expense Report Rules (quick reference)

These are the entry rules that the [SOP](SOP.md) and the Trip Voucher Helper follow. When your approver gives new guidance, add it here.

## Taxes
| Expense | How to enter tax |
|---|---|
| Material, power tools, printing & copying, equipment | Tax on its **own line**. These are the only cases. |
| Airfare | **One line** at the total fare, taxes included |
| Hotel | All taxes in the lodging line's **Tax box**, whatever the folio calls them ("daily fee", "lodging fee", convention/tourism fee, occupancy tax). Only an obscure charge (for example a $200 resort fee) gets its own line. Don't book resorts. |
| Parking | **One line**: (daily cost + daily tax) × number of days |

## Lodging
- Claim the **folio** nightly room rate (the receipt), capped at the GSA lodging rate. Never claim the per diem rate in place of the receipt.
- In Unanet's lodging per diem wizard, the GSA rate is the allowance and the folio rate is the daily rate.
- Book at or under per diem. If a stay looks likely to cost more, pick a different hotel with a kitchen.

## Meals and incidentals (M&IE)
- GSA M&IE rate for the job site's locality. No receipt.
- **75% only** on:
  - the **first day**: home to the first job site;
  - the **last day**: last job site back home.
- **Every other day is a full day (100%).**
- **Continuous travel**: if you travel from one job site straight to another, that day is claimed **once, on the new trip, at 100% of the new site's** rate.
  - In the Travel tracker, write `M&IE full day (continuous travel site to site)` in that meals row's Business purpose. The tracker's GSA M&IE check then allows 100% on a trip's start or end date.
  - In the app: on the old trip tick "My last travel day goes straight to the next job site". On the new trip tick "I traveled straight here from another job site".
- A free hotel breakfast is **not** deducted. A government-provided meal is.

## Receipts
- Real photos or screenshots of the actual receipt only. **Never a typed transcription.**
- Clear and readable. Retake anything blurry.
- **PNG only, no JPEG.** Convert a JPEG or HEIC phone photo to PNG once, then use the PNG.
- Emailed receipts: a screenshot of the original email, or the vendor's PDF.
- Amazon: the Order Summary print page that lists every item and price.
- Rideshare: the trip receipt, not the charge summary. Hotel: the final folio.

## The PDF
- Exactly **one PDF** per report, attached as the only attachment.
- Page 1 is the report summary. Every following page is one receipt, with nothing added to it.
- Images embedded losslessly (no JPEG re-compression).
- Name: `<First initial>. <Last name> <report#>.pdf`. Use the same name in Unanet, the trip folder and Drive.

## Comments
- Leave the Comments box empty on every line. Add a comment only when your approver asks for specific text.
- Unanet's auto-filled wizard summary (vendor, location and so on) is fine to leave.

## Approvals
- Above-market fares, fares over per diem, and purchases of material, tools or equipment need your manager's written approval. Keep it with the trip.
- If an overweight bag charge is likely (a heavy toolbox), say so in the same request.

## In Unanet
- Save, review, then **submit it yourself**.
- Never click **Edit** on a report in approval. It resets the report to "In Use" and has to be resubmitted.
