Receipts stay in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded unless you press “Read receipts”.

Trip details

These set the per diem math and the PDF name. Look up your rates on gsa.gov per diem rates by ZIP for the month you travel.

Trip trackers

Picking up a trip you started? Load your Travel and Overhead tracker spreadsheets (in Google Sheets: File → Download → Microsoft Excel .xlsx) and your receipts .zip. First trip? Skip this. Your trackers are made for you when you save.

Receipts

    Trackers and receipts

    Saves your Travel tracker, plus your Overhead tracker if you have purchases (tools, material, training), with this trip filled in. Formulas and dropdowns stay as they are. Save the receipt PNGs too: next time, load both here to pick up where you left off.

    After you save it

    1. Enter each line from Review in Unanet. Leave Comments empty.
    2. Attach this PDF as the report’s only attachment.
    3. Save the report, look it over, and submit it yourself.
    4. Put the updated trackers back in Google Sheets: open the tracker, File → Import → Upload, pick the file, choose Replace spreadsheet. First time: upload the file to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets.
